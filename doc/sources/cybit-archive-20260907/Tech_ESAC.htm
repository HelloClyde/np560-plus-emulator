      Embedded Speech/Audio Coder (ESAC)
     
    ESAC is useful for high-quality digital recording and playback of speech; and for bandwidth-efficient wireless voice communication applications.
     
    ESAC is available in three signal bandwidths: 4, 5,and 7-kHz, with corresponding sample rate at 8, 11.025, and 16 kHz, respectively. ESAC offers multiple user-selectable data rates. By "embedded operation", the lower-rate coding can be easily achieved by dropping bits from the higher-rate coding.
     
       ESAC-4: 4-kHz bandwidth speech and audio compression.      (Demos)
    FEATURES
      Scalable data rates:3.6/4.8/6.0/7.2/8.4/9.6/10.8/12/13.2/14.4 kbit/s.
      Corresponding compression factors: 35.5/27.8/22.8/19.3/16.8/14.8/13.3/12.0/11.0/10.1.
      At 4.8 kbit/s, achieves near-toll speech quality (estimated Mean Opinion Score, or MOS: 3.8), and stores 27.7 minutes of speech on a 1-MB flash.
      At 9.6 kbit/s and above, achieves good audio quality.
      Good reproduction of tones and acoustic noise.
      Robust speech/audio quality under acoustic background noise.
      Robust to bit errors with added proprietary transmission protection.
      Optional silence compression (voice activity detection with clip prevention).
      I/O format: 16 bit/sample linear PCM, 8-kHz sample rate.
    SPECIFICATIONS
      Timing:
        Encoder algorithmic delay: 25 ms.
        Encoder/decoder processing delay: DSP speed dependent. Total processing delay = 3 to 4.5 ms on 100 MIPS DSP.
       
      TI320C54X DSP:
        Encoder: 12~23 MIPS. 2.1-kword RAM. 6.6-kword ROM.
        Decoder: 1.2~1.4 MIPS. 1.5-kword RAM. 5.4-kword ROM.
        Encoder and Decoder: 13.2-24.4 MIPS. 3.0-kword RAM. 8.4-kword ROM.
       
      Pentium:
        Encoder: 7.6~12.6% (PII450) or 32.8~55.3% (P166) CPU time.
        Decoder: 1.8~2.0% (PII450) or 9.5~10% (P166) CPU time.
