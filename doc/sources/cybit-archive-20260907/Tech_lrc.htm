      Low Rate Coder (LRC)
     
    LRC is useful for high-compression-ratio (long duration) digital recording and playback of speech; and for bandwidth-efficient wireless voice communication applications.
     
    LRC is available in two signal bandwidths: 4 and 5-kHz (LRC-4 and LRC-5), with corresponding sample rate at 8 and 11.025 kHz, respectively. LRC-5 offers wider-bandwidth voice with enhanced intelligibility for applications including language learning devices.
     
       LRC: Speech compression with over 100:1 compression ratio.
    FEATURES
      Scalable data rates:
        - LRC-4: 0.8/0.9/1.0/1.2/1.4/1.6/1.8/2.0/2.4/2.8 kbit/s.
        - LRC-5: 1.66/2.21/3.3 kbit/s.
      LRC-4:
        -At 2 kbit/s, achieves high communication quality with Mean Opinion Score (MOS) = 3.2.
        -At 1.2 kbit/s, stores 112 minutes of speech on a 1-MB flash.
      Robust speech quality under acoustic background noise.
      Optional silence compression (voice activity detection with clip prevention).
      I/O format: 16 bit/sample linear PCM. 8 kHz (LRC-4) and 11.025 kHz (LRC-5) sample rate.
    SPECIFICATIONS
      LRC-4: 4-kHz bandwidth speech       (Demos)
      Timing:
        Encoder algorithmic delay: 48.75~98.75 ms.
        Encoder/decoder processing delay: DSP speed dependent. Total processing delay = 3 to 4.5 ms on 100 MIPS DSP.
       
      TI320C54X DSP:
        Encoder: 10.5~35 MIPS. 3-kword RAM. 11.2-kword ROM.
        Decoder: 4 MIPS. 1.7-kword RAM. 7-kword ROM.
        Encoder and Decoder: 13.5~38 MIPS. 3.6-kword RAM. 12.6-kword ROM.
       
      Pentium:
        Encoder: 7.6~12.6% (PII450) or 32.8~55.3% (P166) CPU time.
        Decoder: 1.8~2.0% (PII450) or 9.5~10% (P166) CPU time.
       
      LRC-5: 5-kHz bandwidth speech       (Demos)
      Timing:
        Encoder algorithmic delay: 42.62~71.7 ms.
        Encoder/decoder processing delay: DSP speed dependent. Total processing delay = 3 to 4.5 ms on 100 MIPS DSP.
       
      TI320C54X DSP:
        Encoder: 22~42 MIPS. 3.1-kword RAM. 11.4-kword ROM.
        Decoder: 11.5~12 MIPS. 1.8-kword RAM. 7.0-kword ROM.
        Encoder and Decoder: 33.5~54 MIPS. 4.0-kword RAM. 13.7-kword ROM.
       
       LRC/VX: Combined Voice Compression and Transformation.      (Demos)
       
      LRC/VX integrates two new functions into LRC: Voice conversion (among male, female, children and robot voices) and speaking speed control. LRC/VX is useful for language learning devices, e-dictionary, toys, children books, text-to-speech, and many other applications. Importantly, the added functions require negligible increase in DSP resources (MIPS, RAM and ROM). For details of voice transformation and speaking speed control, please see Signal Manipulation.
