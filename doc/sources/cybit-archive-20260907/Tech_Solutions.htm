      Technology Licensing
     
    CYBIT develops state-of-the-art proprietary technologies in digital audio and speech processing. These technologies, offered in software, DSP firmware or value-added chips, provide our customers differentiating edge to compete in the marketplace.
     
    Audio Processing
               Hybrid Audio/Speech Coder (HASC)
              High quality audio coding and good quality speech coding.
     
               Music Beat Tracking (BT)
              Robust, accurate, real-time beat tracking for music interactive applications.
     
               Music Manipulation (MM)
              Key shift, tempo control, sound effect (echo) and music styling (equalization, EQ).
     
    Speech Processing
                Low-Rate Coder (LRC)
              Speech coding with > 100:1 compression ratio. Also LRC/VX - LRC with voice transform.
     
               Embedded Speech/Audio Coder (ESAC)
              Toll quality speech coding and good quality audio coding.
     
               Speech Recognition (SR)
              Robust, accurate speech recognition with minimal training and applicable to all languages.
     
               Speaker Verification (SV)
              Reliable biometric voice access control with minimal training.
     
               Voice Transform (VX)
              Voice conversion for male, female, children, robot for games/toy/security applications.
     
               Playback Speed Control
              Voice and music playback speed control without distortion.
