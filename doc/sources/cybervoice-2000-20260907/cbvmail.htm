    What is CyberVoice E-mail?
           CyberVoice Email v2.0 for Windows 95, 98 and NT
       
      CyberVoice E-mail replaces typed e-mail messages with direct speech (your voice!). CyberVoice E-mail allows you to add a personal touch and emotion to your messages, and allows you to compose and comprehend e-mail messages much faster. Additionally, for those who have difficulty with typing or with writing in English, CyberVoice E-mail provides a convenient human-machine interface that eliminates language barriers.

      CyberVoice E-mail functions just like telephone messaging (and can be used for long-distance dictation, Internet chat, and so on). However, it costs you just one local telephone call (to connect to your Internet service provider). There is no long distance call charges - whether you call domestically or abroad.

      The voice messages are compressed to a very small fraction of their original file size using a proprietary, state-of-the-art CyberVoice Compression technology, with a compression ratio of 25.6:1. For a one-minute speech, CyberVoice compression reduces data size from 960 kB down to 37.5 kB. The compressed voice messages, together with optional text and images, are then sent via a user-selected e-mail system over the Internet. The very small size of compressed voice messages makes Internet transmission and computer storage very efficient and economical. Once received, the voice messages can be played back, with high quality and in real time, with just a single click of the mouse.

      Major Features
      Voice messages sent via user-selected e-mail system, such as Microsoft Exchange, Netscape Mail, Eudora, AOL, CompuServe, cc:Mail, and so on.
      Convenient voice message recording and editing (for arbitrary voice insertion and erasure).
      Two recording length limits to choose from according to your preference.
      Toll-Quality digital voice compression at low data rate (5 kbit/s, a 25.6:1 compression).
      Player for real-time voice playback attached and sent to the receiving party for automatic setup.
      High-quality fast voice playback for voice message scanning and slow playback for message pinpointing.
      Wave (.WAV) voice input of any sample rate and sample size, and in stereo or mono format.
      OLE (Object Linking and Embedding) technology for multimedia e-mail (voice, text, image, audio, etc.).
       

     
    Tutorial for Using CyberVoice E-mail
           Record Your Voice
      Click the recording button and start speaking into the microphone. To stop recording, just click the "Stop" button . To insert or overwrite voice message, make sure the "Insert" or "Overwrite" radio button is checked. To erase portions of voice message, click the "Erase before", "Erase after", or "Erase between" buttons.


       

           Send your voice
      Before sending your voice e-mail, make sure that you have
      established the connection to the Internet;
      selected an e-mail system (under the menu "Options");
      typed the E-mail Host information (also under the menu "Options");
      typed the recipient's address and subject (see the following section 3).

      Then after recording and editing your voice message (and typing the text message - see also section 3), click the "Send" button to send your voice message via your selected e-mail system. If this is the first time you send voice e-mail to someone who dose not have the CyberVoice E-mail software, you must attach the CyberVoice player to the voice message by clicking the "Attach player" button  before you click "Send".

      To send a pre-recorded voice message (a .WAV file), make sure they are converted into the format: linear PCM, 8-kHz sample rate, 16-bit/sample, mono.
       

           Select and Use Your Existing E-mail System
      To select an e-mail system, go to the "Options" menu and choose the "Select your E-mail system" menu item. The following dialog will pop up. If your e-mail system is not on the list, select "Others". Make sure the e-mail system you select really exists in your system.

      For MAPI (messaging applications programming interface) compliant e-mail systems (such as Microfoft Exchange, MS Mail, Eudora Pro, Eudora Light, and so on), after you click the "Send" button , the voice message will be automatically embedded into the mail body. You can then send it out as a traditional e-mail.

    CyberVoice E-mail FAQ
           What do I need in order to use CyberVoice E-mail?
      You only need a personal computer with a sound card (with speakers and microphone) and a Windows 95 or NT operating system. No special hardware is needed.
       
           Does the person receiving my voice message need CyberVoice E-mail?
      No. CyberVoice player (to playback your voice message) can be attached to your voice message and sent to the person (with automatic setup) who does not have CyberVoice E-mail software. And this only needs to be done once.
       
           What E-mail system should I use to send CyberVoice E-mail?
      You send CyberVoice E-mail with your favorite existing e-mail system, just like sending a traditional e-mail.
